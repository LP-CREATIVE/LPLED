Fan Zone: Your School’s Videoboard, Live on Every Fan’s Phone

Fan Zone is our free mobile app for schools with an LPLED videoboard. Fans follow the live score from their seat, vote in polls the moment they hit the board, and claim deals from the local businesses backing their team.

App preview shown with demo players and sponsors.

What fans get

  • Live score. The score, clock, down and distance, scoring by quarter, and team stats, straight from the scoreboard at the game. Fans see the same numbers the board is showing.
  • Team. The roster with positions and bios, plus the full schedule with results.
  • Play. When the press box puts a poll, trivia question, or crowd meter on the videoboard, it opens on every fan’s phone at the same moment. Fans vote and watch the tally move live.
  • Shop. Deals from the local businesses that sponsor the team. Fans show the offer at the counter to claim it.
  • Board. A message board for the school’s fans, with reporting and blocking built in.
  • Points and levels. Voting, claiming deals, and posting earn points, and fans climb their school’s leaderboard.

Why it matters for schools

A videoboard only reaches the people looking at it. Fan Zone puts the board on every phone in the stands, so the student section, the parents on the far side, and the fans following from home all see the same game, the same polls, and the same sponsors.

Fan Zone is free for schools. It connects to the scoreboard software that runs our boards, so the score and the polls come straight from your press box.

More value for your sponsors

Every poll can be “presented by” a sponsor, and every deal in the Shop tab sends a fan to a sponsor’s counter. That gives sponsors something they can see working, not just a logo on the board, which makes them easier to sign and easier to renew. See how a scoreboard pays for itself with sponsors.

Where it is launching

Fan Zone launches first at the Tennessee schools already on our videoboard network: Riverdale, Siegel, Smith County, Gordonsville, Jackson County, Seymour, and Friendship Christian. It covers football, baseball, softball, soccer, and basketball, depending on the boards at each school.

The app is in review with Apple and Google now. We will link the App Store and Google Play here as soon as it is live.

Questions

Is Fan Zone free?

Yes. The app is free to download for fans and free for schools.

My school is not listed. Can we get it?

Fan Zone is built for schools with an LPLED videoboard. If your school is planning a new board, request a quote and ask about Fan Zone, or have your athletic director call us.

Who can use it?

Anyone 13 or older with an email address. Fans create an account, pick their school and team, and they are in.

Are the scores official?

Scores come from the scoreboard operator at the game, so they match the board. They are not official records.
